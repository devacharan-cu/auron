Regulation · what this site does and doesn't claim

  • No registration, so no advertising. Under Section 3 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, a project may not be advertised, marketed or sold before it is registered with the state authority. Rainfold isn't registered and isn't offered: there are no prices, no availability and no booking.
  • No lookalike registration number. Where a K-RERA number would appear, this site says "Not registered". It never imitates the real number format.
  • Karnataka's advertising guidelines. K-RERA lists "Guidelines for Releasing of advertisement in print and Electronic media" among its circulars (rera.karnataka.gov.in, checked 12 Oct 2026). A third-party copy of that circular (RERA/ADM/CR-4/2019-20, 14 Nov 2019; IBC Laws) requires the registration number at the top right in visual ads, at least half the size of the project name, plus the K-RERA website address, and bars "subject to change" disclaimers. A real version of this site would follow it, and check the official PDF first.
  • No QR-code rule found. No K-RERA circular title mentions QR codes (checked 12 Oct 2026). Other states have such rules, so a real project should check again.
  • Carpet area first. Areas are carpet areas as the Act defines them, with verandahs, terraces and gardens listed separately.

This is a design portfolio's reading of public sources, not legal advice.

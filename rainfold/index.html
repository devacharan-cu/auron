RainfoldOverviewConcept · fictional

Forty-eight homes, one valley.

Rainfold is a concept for a small community on the two slopes of a shallow fold in the plateau north of Bengaluru. The homes follow the contour, every one looks across the valley, and the valley floor is kept as a shared garden that holds the rain.

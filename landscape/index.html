LandscapePlaces · planting · water

A valley that holds its rain.

The floor of the fold belongs to everyone. Instead of a clubhouse and a list of amenities, Rainfold keeps one continuous landscape: a walk under rain trees, pools that fill in June, courtyards between the terraces and gardens on the lower roofs.

Illustrative concept. A real project would define its amenities, maintenance and access in its registration documents.

Places on the valley floor

  1. 1

    The valley walk

    A shaded path along the whole valley, about 90 m, linking both terraces and the rain garden.

  2. 2

    The rain garden

    A chain of shallow pools. In the monsoon they hold the roof and slope run-off; in the dry months, reeds and stones.

  3. 3

    Rain-tree grove

    Wide, low canopies over the valley floor: the coolest place on the site in the afternoon.

  4. 4

    Courtyards

    Between the terraces: the entrance to every home, and shade on its bedroom side.

  5. 5

    Planted roofs

    The lower terraces' roofs are gardens, seen from the homes above.

The same ground, twice a year.

PrivacyLast reviewed 12 Oct 2026

Nothing is collected.

This website is a design-portfolio prototype for a fictional developer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no forms that send data.

What stays in your browser

To keep the drawings consistent from page to page, the site stores one small entry in your browser's local storage, named auron-state-v1. It holds the time and day shown on the drawings, the home you last selected, your preferred area units and up to three homes you chose to compare. It never leaves your device. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Cookies and tracking

None. The fonts and images are served from this site itself, so no third party learns that you visited.

The visit page

The visit page asks for no personal details. Its request button only shows a message; nothing is sent.

Links

Image credits and sources link to other websites, such as Wikimedia Commons and news publishers, which have their own policies.

Auron and Rainfold are fictional. There is no data controller to contact because no personal data is processed.

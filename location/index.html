North of the city, on the plateau.

Rainfold has no real site. It imagines the kind of land found between the north of Bengaluru and the airport: gently folded ground, granite hills on the horizon and a chain of old tanks. This page describes that region honestly, without travel times to a place that doesn't exist.

Places in the region

  • Hebbal

    The northern gateway of the city, at its lake and the highway north.

  • Jakkur and Yelahanka

    Lakes, older neighbourhoods and an air force station, with farmland beyond.

  • Devanahalli and the airport

    Kempegowda International Airport and the land around it, where much of the north's recent growth has gone.

  • Nandi Hills

    The granite hills on the northern horizon, where the plateau rises.

  • Rainfold (concept)

    Shown as a hatched area, not a point: a kind of place, not a parcel.

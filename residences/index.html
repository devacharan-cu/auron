Four kinds of home, all facing the valley.

Every home is one floor, 12 m wide and 16 m deep, with the valley in front and a courtyard behind. What changes from level to level is what is outside: a garden, the view, or a roof terrace of your own.

Areas, plainly.

Carpet area is the area you live in: inside the walls, measured the way the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 defines it. It excludes the verandah, terraces and gardens, which are listed separately. No super built-up figure is shown anywhere on this site. Why carpet area first →
